About the destination

Welcome to Palawan

Palawan is a province in the western Philippines known for its islands, clear seas, limestone landscapes, rich marine life, and welcoming communities. It offers opportunities for island hopping, snorkeling, kayaking, nature trips, and cultural experiences.

Our mission: To inspire responsible travel by sharing useful destination information and encouraging visitors to respect Palawan's natural and cultural heritage.

Our vision: To help travelers discover Palawan through memorable, safe, and environmentally mindful experiences.

This educational tourism website is designed for students, families, couples, and adventure seekers planning a visit.

Travel smart

Your Palawan Travel Guide

A few reminders to help make your visit comfortable, safe, and respectful of the environment.

☀️

Best Time to Visit

Many travelers prefer the drier months, often around November to May. Weather can change, so check forecasts and sea conditions before departure.

🧴

What to Bring

  • Light clothing and swimwear
  • Sun protection and reusable water bottle
  • Waterproof bag and personal medication
  • Cash and valid identification
🚐

Getting There

Travelers commonly fly to Puerto Princesa, El Nido, or Busuanga (for Coron), depending on the itinerary. Routes and schedules vary; verify them with transport providers.

🐠

Protect Nature

Never touch or stand on coral, avoid feeding wildlife, take all rubbish with you, and follow local marine sanctuary rules.

🛟

Safety First

Follow guides and lifeguards, wear a life jacket when required, check weather advisories, and avoid swimming in restricted areas.

💰

Budget Planning

Budget separately for airfare, accommodation, food, local transport, tours, entrance fees, and emergency funds. Request current quotations before travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book tours in advance?

Advance booking can help during busy periods. Confirm the operator's credentials, inclusions, cancellation terms, and current local requirements.

Are tour package prices final?

No. The prices shown here are sample estimates for an academic project and are not live quotations.

What if the weather is bad?

Follow official advisories and the guidance of local authorities or tour operators. Boat trips may be delayed or cancelled for safety.
